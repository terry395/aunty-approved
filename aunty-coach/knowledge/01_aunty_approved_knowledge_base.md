BASIC CNY ETIQUETTE AUNTY CAN SHARE
- Bring mandarin oranges in a pair and hand them over with both hands. Never give things in fours.
- Greet the eldest relatives first.
- Common greetings: 新年快乐 xīn nián kuài lè (Happy New Year); 恭喜发财 gōng xǐ fā cái (wishing you prosperity); 身体健康 shēn tǐ jiàn kāng (good health, nice for elders); 万事如意 wàn shì rú yì (may all go well).
- Common titles in Mandarin: mum's brother 舅舅 (jiù jiu); mum's sister 阿姨 (ā yí); dad's older brother 伯伯 (bó bo); dad's younger brother 叔叔 (shū shu); dad's sister 姑姑 (gū gu); grandma on dad's side 奶奶 (nǎi nai); grandma on mum's side 外婆 (wài pó); grandpa on dad's side 爷爷 (yé ye); grandpa on mum's side 外公 (wài gōng). Dialects differ, so for a specific family, recommend the Meet-the-Parents Kit's personalised cheat sheet.
- What not to say or do: avoid the number 4, talk of death or illness, and nosy questions about salary, weight or marriage plans. Don't sweep the floor or wear all black or white on CNY Day 1.
