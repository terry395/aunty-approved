Our story

Every first visit, same panic.

You've been together a while, and now it's happening: your first CNY at their family's house. Suddenly it's 2am and you're googling. How many oranges? Which red packet? Is that lady 大姨 (dà yí) or just "Aunty"? And is 舅舅 (jiù jiu) mum's brother or dad's?

Aunty has seen it all. The boyfriend who called Ah Ma "auntie". The girlfriend who brought four of something. (Don't, ah.) Nobody tells you the rules, but everybody notices when you get them wrong.

So we packed everything into one kit: the right gifts, the right words, the right titles. You just need to show up, smile and say 新年快乐. Don't worry, Aunty got you.

    FAQ

    Aunty answers your questions

    When is the last day to order?

    Order by 30 Jan 2027, 23:59 (Singapore time) for guaranteed delivery by 3 Feb, before CNY Eve on 5 Feb. Orders close completely after that. Early-bird prices end on 31 Dec 2026, 23:59.

    Do you deliver? Can I self-collect?

    We deliver island-wide for a flat S$4.90, and it's free for orders S$50 and above (after early-bird discounts). We're delivery only, so there's no self-collection. You pick a delivery date between 26 Jan and 3 Feb 2027.

    Can I buy it as a gift for my partner?

    Of course, so sweet! At checkout, switch on "This is a gift for someone else", enter your partner's name, mobile and address, and add a short gift message (up to 150 characters). We'll deliver straight to them.

    How does the personalised cheat sheet work?

    It comes with the Meet-the-Parents Kit. At checkout you tell us the family's dialect (Hokkien, Cantonese, Teochew or Mandarin) and describe the family, e.g. "her mum has 2 younger brothers and 1 older sister". Aunty Coach and our team map each relative to the right title, with Chinese characters and pronunciation, and check every title by hand before printing. If anything is unclear, we'll contact you before we print.

    Will the oranges stay fresh?

    Yes. We pack the mandarin oranges close to dispatch in late January, so they reach you fresh by 3 Feb. Keep them somewhere cool and dry. And yes, they always come in a pair, as they should.

    How does payment work?

    Aunty Approved is a student project, so the PayNow screen at checkout is a demo. No money is charged, and we never ask for card or bank details. Please don't send real payments.

    Can I cancel my order?

    Yes, free of charge, any time before dispatch (until 25 Jan 2027). Just email hello@auntyapproved.sg (opens in a new tab) with your order number. Personalised cheat sheets that have already been printed can't be cancelled. See for details.
